HOAI Content Studio

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HOAI Content Studio handles information when you connect and use social publishing integrations such as TikTok.

Effective date: August 16, 2026

1. Information we may process

When you authorize a supported social account, HOAI Content Studio may process basic account information returned by the platform, authorization credentials or tokens, publishing status, and content you choose to upload or publish.

2. TikTok data

For a TikTok connection, the app may request permissions needed to identify the authorized account and to upload drafts or publish content. This information is used only to provide the user-requested connection and publishing functionality.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare or transfer user-selected content, submit publishing requests, display publishing status, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.

4. No sale of personal information

HOAI Content Studio does not sell personal information or TikTok user data to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data storage and security

Where credentials or tokens are stored, they are used only for the connected publishing workflow and should be kept on systems controlled by the authorized user or service operator. Reasonable measures are used to protect access to credentials and account data.

6. Sharing

Information is shared with a connected platform only as necessary to perform actions requested by the user, such as authentication, uploading, or publishing. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the service.

7. Retention

Authorization and publishing information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the integration, maintain publishing history, troubleshoot problems, or meet legal obligations. Data that is no longer needed may be deleted.

8. Revoking access and deletion requests

You may revoke access to a connected TikTok account through TikTok's account or security settings when available. You may also request deletion of information associated with HOAI Content Studio through hoaidang.com.

9. Third-party privacy practices

TikTok and other connected platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. This policy covers HOAI Content Studio's handling of information and does not replace the privacy policy of a connected platform.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as HOAI Content Studio and its integrations evolve. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests related to HOAI Content Studio, contact us through hoaidang.com.
